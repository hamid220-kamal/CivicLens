# Civic Knowledge Base Sources (RAG)

CivicLens uses genuine, publicly verifiable civic knowledge sources to ground AI classification, severity estimation, and departmental routing.

---

## Verified Civic Knowledge Sources

| Source Title | Publisher / Authority | Category | Official URL | Usage in CivicLens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Municipal Citizen Charter & Service Standards** | Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs / Municipal Corporation | Department Responsibilities & SLAs | [https://mohua.gov.in](https://mohua.gov.in) | Routing potholes, drainage, and streetlights to exact municipal wings with SLA days. |
| **Solid Waste Management Rules & Guidelines** | Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) | Sanitation & Waste | [https://cpcb.nic.in](https://cpcb.nic.in) | Routing illegal dumping, hazardous waste, and bin overflow to Sanitation & Public Health. |
| **Urban Road Maintenance & Pothole Guidelines (IRC:SP:77)** | Indian Roads Congress | Roads & Infrastructure | [https://irc.nic.in](https://irc.nic.in) | Severity scoring for road defects, sinkholes, and structural hazards. |
| **Standard 311 Civic Issue Taxonomy** | Open Civic Data Standards / Public Works | Issue Taxonomy & Triage | [https://open311.org](https://open311.org) | Standardizing issue types (Pothole, Broken Streetlight, Water Main Break, Garbage Dump). |
| **National Disaster Management Guidelines for Urban Flooding** | National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) | Emergency & Public Safety | [https://ndma.gov.in](https://ndma.gov.in) | Escalating high-severity drainage blockages and waterlogging to Emergency Response. |

---

## Grounding Principle

* Every department recommendation generated by CivicLens must cite a specific `knowledge_chunk` ID and provide the relevant excerpt.
* If a reported issue does not match any documented municipal guideline, the system explicitly marks it as `unclassified_department` and flags it for human operator review rather than hallucinating an authority.
